# Data reuse

The original experimental data supplied by the study authors in `input/mouse/`,
`input/qpcr/`, `input/gcms/`, `input/biolog/`, `input/flow/` and
`input/microbiota/`, and the study-derived data in `cache/` and `expected/`,
are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license
(CC BY 4.0), to the extent the study copyright holders hold rights in them.
The software license in LICENSE applies to code and its documentation, not
as a blanket license over all data.

Credit the study “Resource-access breadth marks protective strains against
toxigenic Clostridioides difficile” and its authors. Cite the eventual paper
and versioned repository release, retain the file-level provenance in
`input_manifest.tsv`, link to CC BY 4.0, and indicate modifications. The
synthetic alignment fixture is a test artifact, not an experimental observation.

## Genomes and external material

`input/genomes/` contains supplied bacterial assemblies and public reference
assemblies. Their accession numbers, sources and roles are documented in
`GENOME_ACCESSIONS.md` and `input_manifest.tsv`. This release does not assert
new ownership or replace the original submitters' rights with CC BY or MIT.
Credit the original sequence depositions and use their applicable terms.
NCBI places no restrictions of its own on distribution of molecular data,
but notes that submitters may hold rights; see
https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/about/policies/#molecular.

Externally installed MATLAB/toolboxes and genomics programs retain their own
licenses. No software executables or vendor libraries are bundled here.
