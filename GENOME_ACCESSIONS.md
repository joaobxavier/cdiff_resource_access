## Mapping to package inputs

All FASTAs are in `input/genomes/`. Clinical isolates use `ST1_N.fasta`, where
N is the isolate number (for example, ST1-75 uses `ST1_75.fasta`).
The reference files are:

- GCF_000027105.1: `GCF_000027105.1_ASM2710v1_genomic.fna`.
- GCF_015238635.1: `GCF_015238635.1_ASM1523863v1_genomic.fna`.
- GCA_001995155.1: `GCA_001995155.1_VPI10463.fasta`.

The ST1 assemblies were supplied in Vishwas's shared-drive Chapter 4 collection,
not reconstructed from the public reads for this analysis. The public-read
mapping is based on exact, unique ENA sample aliases (ST1_N); it does not claim
a verified read-to-assembly reconstruction. Source provenance and exact
FASTA SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `input_manifest.tsv`.
The two RefSeq references were downloaded from NCBI by accession; the
alternative VPI10463 assembly was supplied on the shared drive and matched to
GCA_001995155.1.

Metadata verified on 2026-09-20 against the ENA run report for PRJNA595724 and
NCBI assembly reports. PRJNA885086, also cited by Dong et al., contains engineered
R20291 derivatives and is not the source project for these 21 clinical isolates.
